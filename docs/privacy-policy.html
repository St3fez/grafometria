Privacy Policy

HandwritingAnalysis is designed with data minimization: live camera intake, local analysis flow, and user-controlled sharing.

Last updated: March 4, 2026

1) Controller and scope

This policy applies to the Android app HandwritingAnalysis (com.grafometria.grafometria) published by st3fez.

It explains what data is processed when you use the app and what third-party services are involved.

2) Data categories

Category How it is used Retention
Live camera photo Captured only when you start analysis. Used to compute handwriting metrics on-device. Temporary capture files are deleted on a best-effort basis after processing.
Analysis output Displayed in app popup and used to build optional share card images. Kept in volatile in-app session memory until app process is terminated.
Local settings Stores language choice and consent version in local app preferences. Until changed by user, consent is withdrawn, or app data is cleared.
Ad and purchase signals (third parties) Free mode ads via AdMob and Pro unlock billing via Google Play. According to third-party provider policies and local laws.
Shared content (optional) If you tap Share, exported image/text is passed to your selected app (for example WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook). Managed by the selected third-party app according to its own policy.

3) Data we do not intentionally process

  • No gallery or device file-picker intake.
  • No account registration required by this app.
  • No cloud backup of handwriting photos by this app.
  • No biometric identification and no identity verification logic.

4) Purpose and legal basis

  • Provide educational handwriting analysis features.
  • Show ads in Free mode and process Pro purchase requests.
  • Keep basic app settings and consent preferences.

Where GDPR applies, processing is based on: consent for camera/analysis workflow and policy acceptance; and contract necessity for paid Pro unlock transaction handling.

5) Third-party services

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: Google Payments and Play billing terms.
  • External sharing apps process exported content under their own policies.

6) User controls and rights

  • You can withdraw in-app consent from the policy/disclaimer panel.
  • You can stop using camera analysis at any time.
  • You can clear local app data from Android settings to remove local preferences.
  • If regional law grants access/erasure/objection rights, contact the developer through the support channel listed on Google Play.

7) Security and transfer

The app uses standard Android app sandboxing and transport security provided by platform/SDKs. Any international data transfer by third-party providers is governed by their legal mechanisms.

8) Children

The app is not designed as a service for children and should be used under applicable local age and parental guidance rules.

9) Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features, legal obligations, or third-party SDK behavior change. The latest version is published on this page.
