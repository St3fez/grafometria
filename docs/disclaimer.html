Medical and Psychological Disclaimer

HandwritingAnalysis is an educational screening-style app. It is not a diagnostic medical device.

Last updated: March 4, 2026

1) Not diagnosis

Results are not medical, psychiatric, or neurological diagnosis and do not replace professional evaluation.

2) Traffic-light interpretation in app

The app uses a simplified educational indicator system:

Green Balance and relative emotional stability markers.

Orange Elevated tension/vulnerability markers.

Red Cluster of potential risk markers that may deserve professional follow-up.

3) Disease-specific warning

The app does not diagnose Alzheimer disease, Parkinson disease, or any other neurological disorder.

Any mention of scientific literature is for context only and does not convert app output into a clinical test.

4) What to do if you are concerned

  • If a result repeatedly appears concerning, consult a licensed clinician.
  • In urgent or emergency situations, contact local emergency services immediately.

5) Forbidden decision contexts

  • No hiring or firing decisions.
  • No credit, insurance, or legal decisions.
  • No educational admission or disciplinary decisions.
  • No high-impact decisions based solely on this output.

6) No professional relationship

Use of this app does not create a doctor-patient, therapist-client, or legal advisory relationship.
