Scientific references (educational context)

These references provide context for handwriting-related motor and screening research themes.

Last updated: March 4, 2026

References below are educational only. HandwritingAnalysis does not provide diagnosis and must not be used as a standalone clinical tool.

Core references

  • Postuma RB et al. (2015). MDS clinical diagnostic criteria for Parkinson disease. PMID: 26474316
  • Drotar P et al. (2016). Evaluation of handwriting for Parkinson diagnosis. Artificial Intelligence in Medicine. DOI: 10.1016/j.artmed.2016.03.004
  • Handwriting changes in Alzheimer disease review (2023), Journal of Alzheimer Disease. PMID: 35954551

How these references are used in-app

  • To support transparent educational explanations around graphomotor features.
  • To communicate boundaries: correlation or screening signals do not equal confirmed disease.
  • To guide users toward professional follow-up when concern persists.
