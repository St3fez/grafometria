Quick FAQ

Why does the app require camera permission?

The app works with live camera capture only. It does not import handwriting from gallery or file picker.

Are my results saved permanently?

Results are kept in app session memory and disappear when the app process is terminated. Local app settings such as language and consent can persist until you clear app data.

How does Pro unlock work?

Pro unlock is a per-result in-app purchase. In debug/internal testing, unlock behavior can differ from production billing.

Sharing to WhatsApp/Instagram fails on my device. What should I do?

  • Update target apps from Google Play.
  • Retry from the app "Share" tab and also test System Share.
  • Clear target app cache and reboot device if needed.
  • Check target app permissions and media sharing support.

Photo quality tips

  • Use good lighting and avoid shadows over handwriting.
  • Keep camera parallel to paper and avoid perspective skew.
  • Capture full lines with enough margin around text.
  • Hold device steady until focus is sharp.
